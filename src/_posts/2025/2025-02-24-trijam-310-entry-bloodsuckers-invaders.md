Last weekend I participated in **[Trijam #310](https://itch.io/jam/trijam-310)**.

> Trijam is a weekly game jam where your goal is to make something playable (and fun!) in just 3 hours.

{% itchio 3352377 %}

## The theme
The theme for this game jam was **"Vampires"**.

> This was the sixth Trijam that I participated with purpose to test my small game engine: [Blazorame](/tags/blazorame).

The night has fallen, and the creatures of darkness are closing in! Bloodsuckers Invaders is a retro arcade shooter inspired by Space Invaders but with a vampiric twist. Armed with a holy cross-shaped stake, your mission is to drive back wave after wave of bloodthirsty monsters before they overrun the land.

Monsters of the Night:
* 🦇 Blood Bats – Fast and erratic winged creatures.
* 🕴️Dhampirs – Half-human, half-vampire hybrids.
* 🩸 Strigoi – Undead vampires with a thirst for destruction.
* 🕶️ Nosferatu – Shadowy and terrifying.
* 🐺 Werewolves – As dangerous as they are relentless.
* 🧛Dracula – The Dark Lord himself, descending as the final boss. Defeat him to end the invasion, but beware—he’s not an easy target!

The final result is: **Bloodsuckers Invaders**.
