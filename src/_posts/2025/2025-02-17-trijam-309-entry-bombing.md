Last weekend I participated in **[Trijam #307](https://itch.io/jam/trijam-309)**.

> Trijam is a weekly game jam where your goal is to make something playable (and fun!) in just 3 hours.

{% itchio 3330464 %}

## The theme
The theme for this game jam was **"Dangerous Choices"**.

> This was the fifth Trijam that I participated with purpose to test my small game engine: [Blazorame](/tags/blazorame).

In Bombing, you take control of a military plane pilot tasked with bombing enemy military lines while carefully avoiding civilian buildings. Inspired by classic Atari 2600 arcade gameplay, every drop of your bomb is a dangerous choice—will you strike the enemy or cause collateral damage?


Gameplay
* Bomb military buildings (pink) to earn points.
* Avoid hitting civilian buildings—each mistake costs you points.
* Destroy all military buildings to advance to the next level.
* With every level, your plane gets faster, making precision even more critical.
* Game Over if you destroy all civilian buildings or crash into any building.

The final result is: **Bombing**.
