Last weekend I participated in **[Trijam #307](https://itch.io/jam/trijam-307)**.

> Trijam is a weekly game jam where your goal is to make something playable (and fun!) in just 3 hours.

{% itchio 3296230 %}

## The theme
The theme for this game jam was **"Web Of Issues"**.

> This was the fourth Trijam that I participated with purpose to test my small game engine: [Blazorame](/tags/blazorame).

This Atari 2600-style arcade game where you play as a hungry spider, fighting to survive the relentless waves of insects invading your web at night.

🕷️ Survive, Hunt, and Weave!
* Every time you throw a web, you lose a portion of your web (bottom bar).
* Hit a bug with your web to turn it into a cocoon.
* Eat cocoons to regain the web and stay in the fight.
* Bugs that collide with a cocoon also get trapped—use this to your advantage!

The final result is: **Web Of Bugs**.
